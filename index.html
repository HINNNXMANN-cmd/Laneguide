Traffic memory

The app learns how busy roads usually are, by road area and hour of the week, from your own drives. It then uses that, even offline, to colour your route, adjust the arrival time, and prefer a faster alternative.

Data

Search and routing need internet (free OpenStreetMap services). A route you have already calculated keeps working offline with live GPS, and saved map areas still show. Lane guidance uses lane data from OpenStreetMap, and shows turn guidance only when a road has none.
