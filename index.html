Map detail

Traffic memory

The app learns how busy roads usually are, by road area and hour of the week, from your own drives. It then uses that, even offline, to colour your route, adjust the arrival time, and prefer a faster alternative.

Data

What works offline, and what does not

Works with no internet: the map areas you saved, searching saved places, tapping the map for a place card, speed limits, camera and closed-road alerts, your saved places, traffic memory, and a trip you planned before (it keeps guiding you). Needs internet: planning a new route, searching places you have not saved, and rerouting. Closed roads here are roadworks recorded in OpenStreetMap, not live closures, and cameras and speed limits are only as complete as the map data. Alerts and speed limits are aids, not a guarantee; follow the road signs. Maps © OpenStreetMap contributors.
